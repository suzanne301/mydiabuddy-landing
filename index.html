THE REAL PROBLEM

The work doesn't end when a decision is made. It starts again at the next meal, the next reading, the next unexpected change.

What we're exploring

Support that understands the context—not just the number.

DiaBuddy is being shaped as a companion for everyday Type 1 diabetes decisions: a way to bring the factors that matter into one clearer picture and reduce the mental effort of piecing them together.

We are at an early research stage. We are listening first, testing the problem and learning what would genuinely help before making promises about the product.

An underexplored variable

Hormones can change the rules.

Many women report that menstrual cycles, pregnancy, perimenopause and menopause can make glucose patterns and insulin needs feel less predictable.

We want to understand those experiences better—without assuming every woman is affected in the same way, or pretending the answer is simple.

Why I'm building DiaBuddy

Built from lived experience, not from the sidelines.

I'm Suzanne, founder of My DiaBuddy, and I live with Type 1 diabetes. I started DiaBuddy because I wanted the kind of support that recognises the full reality of daily decisions—not just isolated data points.

I don't want to guess what other people need. That's why this stage is about listening: learning where the greatest burden really sits, whose needs are being overlooked and what useful support could look like.

Suzanne Founder, My DiaBuddy
